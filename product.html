SAMS EX‑04Z1

An intrinsically safe Android smartphone engineered for Zone 1 and Zone 2 hazardous environments, purpose-built for oil & gas, petrochemical, mining, power generation, pharmaceutical and marine & offshore operations. It pairs a Qualcomm Snapdragon platform running Android 16 with a reinforced, glove-operable housing rated IP68 and MIL‑STD‑810H, engineered to IECEx, ATEX and NEC 500 for entry into the world’s most demanding hazardous-area sites.

Key Features

  • Android 16, Enterprise-managed operating system
  • Qualcomm Snapdragon, 8-core processing platform
  • 50 MP rear camera with phase-detect autofocus & LED flash
  • 4,000 mAh battery with intrinsically safe charging circuitry
  • Sunlight-readable display with glove & wet-hand touch support
  • 4G LTE, dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth & NFC connectivity
  • Multi-constellation GNSS (GPS · GLONASS) for lone-worker safety
  • IP68 rated — dust-tight & protected against continuous immersion
  • MIL‑STD‑810H durability rated, industrial-grade housing
  • Engineered to IECEx, ATEX & NEC 500 for Zone 1 & Zone 2
